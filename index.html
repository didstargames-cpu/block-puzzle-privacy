Privacy Policy

DIDSTAR ("we") provides Block Puzzle Combo Quest, an Android puzzle game. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the game.

1. Game data

The game stores progress, scores, settings, virtual coins, booster inventory and saved games locally on your device. These are used to operate the game and restore your progress. Android backup settings may also affect retention of local data.

2. Advertising and consent

The game integrates Google AdMob, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security and, where permitted, personalization. Processing depends on your device settings, region and privacy choices. Advertising SDKs may store or access identifiers and consent information on your device.

Where required, consent is requested before advertising requests are permitted. You may decline optional consent or change your choices through the applicable privacy options in the game when available, and manage advertising identifiers and personalization in Android settings. If you cannot access privacy options, contact us. Declining consent may affect ad availability, including optional rewarded ads.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

3. Contact and data sharing

If you email us, we process your email address and the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and advertising partners as described above. We may disclose information where legally required. Third-party processing may take place in countries other than your own.

4. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until removed or overwritten. You can delete it through Android app storage settings or by uninstalling the game, subject to device backup behavior. This may permanently remove your progress. Support communications are retained as reasonably necessary to handle requests and legal obligations. Google and its partners apply their own retention policies to information they process.

5. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, request portability, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. Processing relies on consent where required, provision of requested functionality, legitimate interests such as security and support where permitted, and legal obligations where applicable.

6. Children

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action. Applicable age-related privacy and advertising requirements must be respected.

7. Website and changes

This policy page does not include advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies of its own. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information to deliver and secure the page. See GitHub's privacy statement. We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
